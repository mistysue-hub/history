Numbers Across Time

Mathematics wasn't invented once — it was invented, lost, and reinvented on nearly every continent, for reasons that ranged from taxing grain to mapping the stars to arguing about the nature of infinity. This course follows the idea of number and proof as it moves through Africa, Mesopotamia, Greece, India, China, the Islamic world, the Americas, and modern Europe and beyond.
